# Practice Activity

Individually you will be allocated a context. 

**Step 1:** Write as many words as you can think of that are associated with the context. (3 mins)

**Step 2:** Find any patterns or categories in the words you wrote

**Step 3:** For each category, or for individual items in the list, identify a problem that might occur there. (5 mins)

**Step 4:** Review - are there any problems (or set of problems) that could be solved with an innovative application of emerging technologies? (5 mins)

Once you have completed the cycle for your allocated context -  we will repeat for a context that you choose.

# Turning problems into solutions

You should now have two sets of problems that you have identified.

- Which problems are most interesting?
- Which technology (or combination of technologies) could be applied to solve the problem?

At this point we still don't want to judge the ideas too much: try focus on just getting ideas down.

# Document your progress

We will be documenting our progress in a project log. This will be a document you will use to:

- Demonstrate your progress through the entire project
- Record the evolution of your ideas over time
- Demonstrate your ability to apply basic project management skills
- Keep all your progress in one place for assessment

## In your project log, start by

- Recording the context *you were allocated* and the associated problems you identified
- Recording the context *you chose* and the associated problems you identified
- Recording the emerging technologies you think could be applied to solve the problems you identified

Once you have completed these: you can start identifying solution ideas.
