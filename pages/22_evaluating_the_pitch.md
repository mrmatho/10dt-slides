# Evaluating the Pitch

---
layout: center
---

# Collating the data

**You need to:**

1. Review the responses received in your Google Form.
2. Identify which criteria you performed best and worst.
3. Identify any common themes in the feedback you received and any suggestions for improvement.
4. Select which pieces of feedback you can realistically implement to improve your pitch.

Record what you are doing for inclusion in your report.

---
layout: center
---

# Improving your pitch

Make **2-3 changes** to your pitch based on the feedback you received. These changes should be focused on improving your pitch in areas you can **realistically improve with the time and resources you have available**.

---
layout: center
---

# Writing your report

Your report needs to include:

1. An evaluation of your pitch against the provided criteria. This should include both a summary of the feedback received and your own evaluation. 

    - At least a sentence for each of the criteria.

2. A description of the 2-3 changes you made to your pitch based on the feedback received, and why you made those changes. Also justify why some other suggested changes were not implemented.
3. A summary of how your evaluation against criteria would change since making the improvements. (1 paragraph)
4. A reflection on what you could have done differently from the start of the project.

---
layout: center
---

# Submission

Once your evaluation report is complete, you need to submit the following on Compass:

- Your final pitch presentation
- Your evaluation report
- Your project plan

Any design tools that didn't make it into your final pitch presentation should also be included in your submission.
